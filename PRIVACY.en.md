# Gaveta privacy policy

**English** · [Português](PRIVACIDADE.md)

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

## Data used

When you save a video, Gaveta stores its ID, type (video or Short), title, URL, and the time it was saved. It uses this information only to display, organize, open, and export your queue.

The queue is stored in `chrome.storage.local` in the Chrome profile where the extension is installed. Gaveta does not sync it across computers. It does not collect your full browsing history, passwords, payment details, or content from other websites.

## Sharing and network activity

Gaveta does not send your queue to an Evakdo server and contains no ads or usage analytics. When you open the drawer, saved titles and links appear inside the YouTube page; scripts on that page can technically inspect the displayed content. Thumbnail images are requested from YouTube's `i.ytimg.com` domain.

The footer links to `https://evakdo.com/`. It opens only if you click it, in a new tab, without sending the source page URL as a referrer.

## Your controls

You can remove individual videos from the drawer and download the whole queue as a CSV file. The downloaded file stays under your control; Gaveta does not upload it. Export your list before uninstalling the extension or moving to a different installation if you want to keep it.

## Contact

Once the repository is published, questions and requests about the extension can be submitted through its GitHub **Issues** section.
